## Null Result Pre-Analysis

On a null result for a module, the assumption(s) killed are:
- Primary: "the artifacts that are actually filled in contain structure the tool can surface" — because the corpus
  is thin (≈2–3 independent subjects per module), a null may reflect **corpus size, not the idea**.

On a null result, the following are NOT killed:
- The correctness of the tool (unit / metamorphic / mutation tests are independent of the real-data result).
- The underlying methods (EC², minimal cut sets, metamorphic testing) — they have decades of prior art.
