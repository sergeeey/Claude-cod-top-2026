## Findings and fixes

### F1 — Restrict the range calculation to its endpoint domain

**MEDIUM.** [CODE] [Lines 167–170](https://github.com/sergeeey/Claude-cod-top-2026/blob/216d498910d03bbb43fe61adc18bf8d8bd84e8df/rules/estimand-ops.md#L167) define available change as `baseline` for a decrease and `1 - baseline` for an increase without limiting this to probabilities. The section applies generally to sampling-based comparisons; line 226 explicitly gives a continuous-endpoint formula.

[VERIFIED-SYNTHETIC] Counterexample: centered continuous endpoint, baseline 0, sigma 10, n=30 per arm, two-sided alpha=.05, power=.8. The document's formula gives MDE **7.233662 endpoint units**. The installed NormalIndPower implementation confirms approximately **0.800001** power at that effect under the normal model. The general range instruction instead compares 7.233662 with `1 - 0 = 1` and would label it undetectable. An unbounded continuous endpoint has no such cap. This is a mathematical counterexample, not an observed production incident.

**Impact:** A literal implementation can incorrectly suppress an informative continuous-outcome result or require a larger design.

**Fix:** Restrict these bounds to probability endpoints on [0,1]. For other bounded endpoints use actual support and units; do not impose a probability cap on unbounded outcomes. Also preserve the direction and target power in line 186's “any effect” wording.

### F2 — Require a confidence interval for the actual contrast

**MEDIUM — specification ambiguity, not a reproduced consumer bug.** [CODE] [Lines 190–193](https://github.com/sergeeey/Claude-cod-top-2026/blob/216d498910d03bbb43fe61adc18bf8d8bd84e8df/rules/estimand-ops.md#L190) prescribe an exact or Wilson interval when claiming exclusion of effects at MCID, illustrated with the single count 0/30. They do not explicitly require a two-arm comparison to account for uncertainty in both estimated arms. The general estimand-to-estimator principle elsewhere helps but leaves this local method prescription ambiguous.

[VERIFIED-SYNTHETIC] For 50/100 events in each independent arm, a single-arm Wilson interval shifted by the estimated control proportion is **[-0.096168, 0.096168]**. The Newcombe interval for the two-arm difference is **[-0.136003, 0.136003]**. At MCID=.10, the former would claim exclusion while the latter would not. This illustrates a consequential reading of the ambiguous instruction; no project consumer was shown to have actually made this mistake.

[DOCS] Current primary documentation distinguishes [a binomial proportion interval](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.proportion.proportion_confint.html) from [an interval comparing two independent proportions](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/generated/statsmodels.stats.proportion.confint_proportions_2indep.html), with Newcombe and score methods for differences.

**Fix:** Require an interval for the declared estimand with matching units, direction and design. Reserve single-proportion Wilson/exact intervals for one probability or a truly fixed threshold. Name an appropriate two-proportion contrast interval and direct continuous/paired/other outcomes to matching methods. Never treat an estimated comparator as known without uncertainty.
